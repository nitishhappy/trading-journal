📖 5. VANTAGE EXECUTION CHEAT SHEET
▶
SELL (Short) Execution:
• Entry: Fills at Bid (chart price). Zero spread at open.
• Take Profit: Must add spread (Support + Spread) so order fills the instant the candle touches your support line!
• Stop Loss: Add spread (Resistance + Spread) so Ask doesn't stop you out before testing resistance.
BUY (Long) Execution:
• Entry: Fills at Ask (Entry + Spread). Trade opens in the red by spread.
• SL & TP: Fill directly when the chart candle touches your line.
Spread Conversion Math:
Points between buttons divided by 10^Decimals (e.g. 1706 / 10^2 = 17.06 USD for BTC).
